# Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Regulatory Framework Summary

## Master Direction on Information Technology Governance, Risk, Controls and Assurance Practices (2023)

### Chapter III - Information Security
* **Para 11 (Data Protection & Privacy)**: Mandates encryption in transit and at rest, data classification, and explicit masking of sensitive customer data (Aadhaar, PAN, Bank Account Number, CVV).
* **Para 14 (Cyber Threat Defense)**: Mandates automated perimeter defense and inspection of automated processes against prompt injection and malicious payload delivery.

### Chapter V - Audit & Logging
* **Para 22 (Tamper-Resistant Logging)**: Mandates centralized, synchronized, and cryptographically verified audit trails retained for at least 7 years.

## Digital Payment Security Controls (2021)
* **Section 4**: Strict prohibition of unmasked cardholder PAN and CVV storage across payment intermediaries.
